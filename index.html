Movie Recommender System

May 2023

About 95% of user–movie ratings are missing. This model fills the gaps.

  • Filled missing ratings for cold-start users and movies by linking films in a genre knowledge graph and estimating each gap from the most similar movies.
  • Reached an RMSE of 0.89 on held-out MovieLens 1M ratings, 21% below a neural model alone (1.13), by factorising the filled matrix with SVD (k=25) and weighting each prediction with neural collaborative filtering.

Raft for Faster Reads

Jan – Feb 2024

Crash the leader. Cut off the majority. Lose the whole quorum. The log still never diverges.

  • Cut p99 read latency by about 80% by serving linearizable reads locally under heartbeat-renewed leader leases.
  • Recovered crashed nodes in under 500 ms without split-brain reads by restoring from persisted write-ahead logs, propagating lease time and delaying new-leader commits.
  • Verified zero log divergence on a 5-node GCP cluster by injecting leader crashes, follower restarts, majority isolation and total quorum loss.
